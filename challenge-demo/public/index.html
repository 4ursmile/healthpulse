Better information for earlier planning.

Climate shifts can affect disease patterns and worker safety. HealthPulse’s existing prototype focuses on climate-informed disease forecasts, uncertainty and preparedness simulation.

The challenge proposes extending that foundation with worker and employer partners to understand heat exposure and plan protective action. This extension has not yet been validated.

How the prototype works

Gather

Bring public climate, disease and population data into a traceable pipeline.

Forecast

Compare model predictions with baselines and show uncertainty.

Simulate

Explore resource and demand scenarios using explicit assumptions.

Review

Give planners the evidence to make and document their own decisions.

    Evidence with limits

    The HealthPulse repository reports a retrospective Brazil dengue replay with a median six-week lead against threshold monitoring among 19 states that crossed the threshold, with one false alarm. This is not prospective validation and does not establish worker-heat prediction performance.
