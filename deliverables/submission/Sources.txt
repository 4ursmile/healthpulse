Data rights and sensitivity
OpenDengue V1.3: CC BY 4.0 (repository attribution applies). NASA POWER: public data, cite NASA POWER. OCHA COD-AB: source attribution as supplied in the dataset manifest. WHO GHO workforce/system data used in the project carries CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO in its source manifest. GADM polygons have academic/non-commercial restrictions and are used as a crosswalk, not redrawn on these slides. No individual worker data is used. Public access does not establish unrestricted commercial-use rights.

Software: Preact/htm MIT; MapLibre GL 4.7.1 BSD-3-Clause; IBM Plex 5.1.1 OFL.

Evidence files are internal evaluations. They are not official competition rankings or independent validation. Brazil forecasts are reported probable dengue notifications. Vietnam post-2010 provincial outputs are estimates. The map basis contains 34 provinces/cities. The worker heat-risk extension remains proposed.
